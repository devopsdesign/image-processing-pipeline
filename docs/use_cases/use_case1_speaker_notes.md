# WildEye Speaker Notes

## Opening
“Today I want to present WildEye, a serverless AI solution built for wildlife conservation teams that need to process camera trap images without a large budget.”

## The Problem
“Conservation groups often receive huge volumes of images, but they do not have enough time or staff to review them manually. That means important sightings can be missed.”

## The Solution
“WildEye automates the first layer of analysis by identifying likely animals, humans, and other noteworthy content so volunteers can focus on the most important results.”

## Why the Architecture Matters
“The serverless design is essential here because it means the system only runs when images arrive. That keeps costs low and makes the solution practical for small organizations.”

## Security Message
“Even in conservation use cases, security and responsible handling matter. The architecture is built to be controlled, auditable, and easy to extend with stronger safeguards.”

## Closing
“WildEye shows that serverless AI can make a real difference in the field by helping organizations do more with less.”
